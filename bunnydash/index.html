Need help?

Email bunnydashsupport@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

How do I play?

Tap to hop over obstacles. Double tap for a big hop up onto ledges and branches. Swipe down to drop a level, or swipe down and hold to duck. You can see the controls any time in Settings.

What are golden carrots?

Golden carrots are found up high on ledges and branches, and come from daily gifts, missions and challenge levels. Spend them on power-up uses and new scenes in the Shop. Regular carrots level power-ups up and unlock new bunnies.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. No Ads is restored for free on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Golden carrot packs are consumable and can't be restored once they've been added to your game.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game while connected to the internet; pending purchases are added automatically. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I turn off notifications?

In the game, open Settings and switch off Notifications. You can also turn them off in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Bunny Dash.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

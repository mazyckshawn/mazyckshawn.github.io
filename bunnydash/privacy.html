Bunny Dash Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bunny Dash iPhone game ("Bunny Dash", "we", "us") and the services it uses collect, how it's used, and the choices you have. Bunny Dash has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, carrots, golden carrots, power-ups, bunnies, scenes, missions, challenge progress and settings) is saved only on your iPhone. We do not upload it to our own servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Bunny Dash shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google's use of this information is described in its Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and certain US states), the game shows a consent message from Google before personalized ads can be shown. If you don't allow tracking or personalized ads, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized. You can stop between-round ads entirely by buying No Ads.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check and record purchases so they work reliably and can be restored. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for Bunny Dash, and basic device information (such as device model, OS version, country and app version). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Bunny Dash schedules reminders on your device (for example when your daily gift is ready). These are local notifications created by the app on your iPhone; no push token or notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

How information is used

Information collected by these services is used to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, process and restore purchases, and understand basic usage so we can keep the game working. We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for anything other than running the game.

Children

Bunny Dash is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off ad tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Change your ad consent choices from the consent message when it appears.
  • Turn off notifications in the game's Settings or iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all game data stored on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we don't hold personal information on our own servers, most requests are handled by the services above; contact us and we'll help you with them.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
